A program that writes a two-line explanation of rain to /output/entry.txt. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run" bounty, $2 in USDC.

Line 1 explains rain to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on a single analogy — a cloud letting go of its water drops the way a wet dog shakes water off its fur. Line 2 says why the analogy works: what it maps (drops leaving the cloud when they get too heavy) and what it leaves out (clouds never shake or choose to do it).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), exactly two lines, 347 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline.
